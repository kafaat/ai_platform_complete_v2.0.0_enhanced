# Synthetic review inputs

These files contain invented identifiers, geometry and measurements for local
software checks. They are not Yemeni field evidence, consent records or vendor
exports collected from a customer. Do not seed operational or RAG stores with them.

See `docs/runbooks/CUSTOMER_PRACTICE_DEFERRED_WORK_AR.md` for the two CLI commands,
current owner mapping, supported FieldView subset and the field acceptance steps.

The practice example contains one training candidate and one held-out candidate.
Both are deliberately artificial; preparing a manifest starts no training and
establishes no agronomic validity. Replace inputs only with authorised exports
whose source records, units, availability times, maturity policy and consent
ledger have been checked by their owners.
